Quantum Nexus Ventures builds sovereign, EU-hosted AI for regulated legal and financial work: verifiable citation-fidelity, multi-jurisdiction legal corpora, and auditable, human-in-the-loop decision support. It offers Nexus Legal, Nexus Finance and Grundnorm products, serving 60 jurisdictions across Latin America, Europe, Asia-Pacific and Africa, plus the EU layer. Founded in 2024 in Dubai, the company focuses on RegTech, AML screening, and AI governance.

**Areas:** LegalAI · RegTech · AI · Finance · Dubai

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Quantum Nexus: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
